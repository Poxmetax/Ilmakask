## Part 1 · The shop: Stan Store

**What it costs (checked 25 Sep 2026):** Stan has no free plan. **Creator** is $29/month or $300/year after a 14-day free trial, with 0% Stan transaction fee; Stripe or PayPal still charge their normal processing fee. Creator already includes the storefront, lead magnets (email capture) and Instagram AutoDM, which is everything this launch needs. **Creator Pro** ($99/month) adds discount codes, email broadcasts and flows, upsells and funnels; you do not need it to start.

Compared with a free platform taking 5% per sale, the $29 fee pays for itself at roughly 45 sales a month at $13 (about 58 at the $10 launch price). Below that, it is a fixed cost you are choosing for the link-in-bio store and AutoDM.

**Timing:** start the 14-day trial on **Sun 27 Sep**, the day before the first post, so the trial covers the first two weeks of posting. Stan asks for a card before the first charge. Cancel before day 14 if it is not working.
