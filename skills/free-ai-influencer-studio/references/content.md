# Content engine

## Niche research

Run 3 to 5 searches with the current year in the query: engagement benchmarks by niche; which niches monetise through digital products; top creators (human and AI) in the candidate niche; current hooks and formats in that niche on Reels and TikTok; the audience's live pain points and debates. Extract patterns, not topics to copy.

Choose the niche at the crossing of:
1. **Engagement:** a category that sits above the platform average for its size tier. Benchmarks differ by source and by follower tier, so compare within a tier, and remember engagement and pay are separate rankings (a low-engagement niche can still attract big brand budgets).
2. **A product-shaped pain:** a chronic, felt problem that a $9 to $27 system (plan, workbook, template pack, swipe file) can solve.
3. **A world the user can photograph:** real places within reach, which is also the account's visual moat.

Write the niche as one sentence: "[persona] helps [who] with [pain] through [format], set in [real place]."

## Idea generation

Build 8 ideas on proven skeletons, mixing them: listicle, contrarian take, signs and flags, things I wish I knew, storytime or confession, POV direct address, day-in-the-life, myth-bust, rating countdown, challenge with a win-or-lose ending. Make 1 to 2 of them a numbered series (a part number implies an archive worth following). Make 2 to 3 lead naturally toward the product without being ads. At least 2 must twist a pattern found in the research through the persona's own angle.

Per idea output: title, the hook (first spoken line), format and hold (why they watch to the end), setting (one line usable as a plate brief), why it works (tied to a research pattern, with the source).

Kill filter before output: would a stranger stop in 2 seconds; is the hook specific (a number, a callout, a claim or a curiosity gap); can it be produced as one person in one real place with one simple action; does it stay inside the safety rules.

## Hooks

- Put the most surprising or specific word first. No warm-up, no "so", no "today I want to talk about".
- Draft three hooks per idea silently and ship only the strongest.
- The hook must also work as on-screen text read in about one second, because many people watch muted.
- Specific numbers must be true and sourced (a day length, a step count, an opening year). A false number in a hook is the fastest way to lose trust in comments.

## Script rules (write for the ear and the voice model)

- Clean spoken text only: no symbols, no stage directions, numbers written as spoken.
- Word budget: about 2.3 to 2.6 words per second of speech. A 7 s clip fits 15 to 18 words with a short silent tail; a 10 s clip about 22 to 25. Count them.
- Structure: hook, deliver, payoff, optional one-line CTA pointing to the bio.
- Human-speech kill list: no tidy rule-of-three lists; no thesis sentence that labels the point; allow one self-correction or trail-off in longer scripts; put verbal tics in the boring middle, not on the punchline; include one oddly specific detail in storytimes; uneven sentence lengths.
- Never bait and switch: the script delivers exactly what the hook promised.

## Captions

Formula: one line that restates the hook in different words, one line of value or context, one question that invites a real answer, then the disclosure line (e.g. "AI-generated character · real places") and 3 to 5 specific hashtags. Keywords in the first line help search more than a block of hashtags. For products: "The full [system] is in my bio." Never pretend a product is free if it is paid.

## Series and cadence

A sustainable default for one person with free-tier credits: 3 to 5 Reels per week plus 1 carousel, planned a week ahead, produced in one or two batch sessions timed to daily credit refreshes. Consistency beats volume; a cadence that survives week six is the right one.

## Safety in content

Health: habits, routines and traditions, never cures, treatments or dosages. Money: principles and habits, never guaranteed returns or specific investment instructions. Relationships: self-respect and clarity, never manipulation scripts. Nothing that sexualises the persona, and nothing aimed at minors.
